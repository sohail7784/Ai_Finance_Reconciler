The problem hiding in every finance team's spreadsheet
Every transaction your business processes creates two separate stories — one told by your bank, another told by Razorpay. Fees get deducted. GST gets applied. Refunds go out. Reference numbers get mistyped. One by one, these small gaps pile up into hours of manual Excel work every single month — work that's tedious, error-prone, and easy to get wrong under deadline pressure.

This AI agent does that work in seconds — and instead of just flagging a mismatch, it tells you exactly why it happened, with a confidence score you can trust.
